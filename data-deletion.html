Data Deletion

Kiswahili

Bongo Challenge haina akaunti wala seva — maendeleo yako yote yako kwenye simu yako tu. Kuyafuta:

  1. Fungua Bongo Challenge → Wasifu → Anza upya maendeleo, kisha thibitisha; au
  2. Ondoa (uninstall) app kwenye simu yako.

Hakuna nakala inayobaki kwetu. Viungo vya quiz ulivyotuma kwa marafiki viko kwenye mazungumzo yako (mf. WhatsApp) — vifute huko ukitaka. Taarifa za matangazo za Google zinadhibitiwa kwenye mipangilio ya Android (Google → Matangazo).

English

Bongo Challenge has no account and no server — all your progress is only on your phone. To delete it:

  1. Open Bongo Challenge → Profile → Reset progress and confirm; or
  2. Uninstall the app.

Nothing is kept by us. Quiz links you sent to friends live in your chats (e.g. WhatsApp) — delete them there if you wish. Google advertising data can be managed in Android settings (Google → Ads).
