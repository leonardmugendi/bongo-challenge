Kiswahili

Bongo Challenge ("app") imetengenezwa na LeonTechForge. Sera hii inaeleza taarifa gani app inatumia na jinsi zinavyolindwa.

1. Hakuna akaunti — maendeleo yako yako kwenye simu

Jina la utani, picha ya mnyama (avatar), sarafu, viwango, nyota za Bongo Pop, historia ya michezo, majina ya wachezaji wa sherehe na mipangilio yako vinahifadhiwa kwenye simu yako tu. Hatuna seva wala akaunti, na hatupokei taarifa hizi.

2. Viungo unavyoshiriki (Changamoto ya Urafiki, Linganisha, Matokeo)

Ukitengeneza quiz au kulinganisha chaguo, maswali, majibu yako na jina lako la utani vinawekwa ndani ya kiungo chenyewe. Kiungo hakihifadhiwi kwenye seva yoyote — unakituma wewe mwenyewe (mf. WhatsApp). Rafiki akikifungua, ukurasa wa wavuti (leonardmugendi.github.io) unakisoma ndani ya kivinjari chake tu. Matokeo ya rafiki yanarudi kwako kwa kiungo anachokutumia yeye. Usiweke taarifa za siri kwenye quiz. Kurasa zinapangishwa na GitHub Pages, ambayo inaweza kuhifadhi kumbukumbu za kawaida za seva (kama anwani ya IP) kwa mujibu wa sera ya GitHub.

3. Matangazo (Google AdMob)

Toleo la bure linaonyesha matangazo kupitia Google AdMob. AdMob inaweza kukusanya taarifa za kifaa kama kitambulisho cha matangazo, anwani ya IP na jinsi unavyoingiliana na matangazo, ili kuonyesha na kupima matangazo. Watumiaji wa maeneo yanayohitaji ridhaa (mf. Ulaya/Uingereza) wataulizwa kwanza na wanaweza kubadilisha kwenye Wasifu → "Faragha ya matangazo". Soma: Jinsi Google inavyotumia taarifa.

4. Ununuzi ndani ya app

Premium inanunuliwa kupitia Google Play. Malipo yanashughulikiwa na Google; hatupokei taarifa za malipo yako.

5. Ruhusa

  • Arifa — kikumbusho cha changamoto ya kila siku (unaweza kuzima).
  • Kihisi cha mwendo (accelerometer) — kinatumika tu wakati wa mchezo wa Igiza kutambua unapoinamisha simu. Hakihifadhiwi wala kutumwa popote.
  • Mtetemo — kwa majibu ya mchezo (unaweza kuzima).
  • Intaneti — kwa matangazo, ununuzi na kufungua viungo tu. Michezo yote inachezwa bila intaneti.

6. Kufuta data

Wasifu → "Anza upya maendeleo" hufuta sarafu, viwango na historia; kuondoa app hufuta kila kitu. Maelezo zaidi: Kufuta data.

7. Watoto

App hii haikulengwa watoto chini ya miaka 13.

8. Mabadiliko

Tukibadilisha sera hii, tarehe iliyo juu itasasishwa.

English

Bongo Challenge (the "app") is made by LeonTechForge. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is protected.

1. No account — your progress stays on your phone

Your nickname, avatar, coins, levels, Bongo Pop stars, game history, party player names and settings are stored only on your phone. There is no server and no account; we never receive this information.

2. Links you share (Friendship Challenge, Compare, Results)

When you create a quiz or a comparison, the questions, your answers and your nickname are packed into the link itself. The link isn't stored on any server — you send it yourself (e.g. on WhatsApp). When a friend opens it, the web page (leonardmugendi.github.io) reads it inside their browser only. Your friend's score comes back to you in a link they send you. Don't put private information in a quiz. The pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's own policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions to serve and measure ads. Users in regions that require consent (e.g. EEA/UK) are asked first and can change their choice in Profile → "Ad privacy options". See how Google uses information.

4. In-app purchases

Premium is bought through Google Play. Payment is handled by Google; we never receive your payment details.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications — a daily challenge reminder (you can turn it off).
  • Motion sensor (accelerometer) — used only during the Igiza game to detect when you tilt the phone. Nothing is stored or sent.
  • Vibration — game feedback (you can turn it off).
  • Internet — only for ads, purchases and opening links. All games work offline.

6. Deleting data

Profile → "Reset progress" deletes coins, levels and history; uninstalling deletes everything. More: Data deletion.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
